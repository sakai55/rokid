# Head-motion mini-games (games/)

[中文](README.md) | English | [日本語](README.ja.md)
Translated from the Chinese version (as of 2026-10-29).

Head-motion + photo-based real-world interaction mini-game series · IDs GA01–GA50: head-motion games GA01–GA40 and photo games GA41–GA50. Minimalist games played by moving your head or taking photos to interact with the real world.

- GA01 [nod-count](nod-count/) — Voice-guided 10 slow nods; head down and back to center counts as one
- GA02 [turn-count](turn-count/) — Voice-guided alternating left and right head turns, 5 each for 10 in total
- GA03 [ear-tilt](ear-tilt/) — Tilt ear to shoulder 3 times on each side; hold steady 5 seconds to count
- GA04 [stretch-hold](stretch-hold/) — Alternate looking up and down stretches, hold 5 seconds each, 3 each
- GA05 [neck-clock](neck-clock/) — Turn your head to the spoken clock positions; must return to center at each point
- GA06 [chin-tuck](chin-tuck/) — Small chin tucks held 5 seconds; an 8-rep chin-tuck exercise
- GA07 [steady-hold](steady-hold/) — Keep your head perfectly still for a full 10 seconds to win; move and the count restarts
- GA08 [nod-catch](nod-catch/) — 10 balls fall; nod to step on the line and catch them for points
- GA09 [turn-dodge](turn-dodge/) — Obstacle on the left, dodge to the right; return to center over 10 rounds for points
- GA10 [follow-dot](follow-dot/) — Follow the light dot wherever it jumps; hold steady on it to score
- GA11 [rhythm-nod](rhythm-nod/) — A fixed 16-beat rhythm; nod on the beat to score
- GA12 [memory-seq](memory-seq/) — A direction sequence is spoken; memorize it and reproduce it in order
- GA13 [find-target](find-target/) — A direction is spoken; turn to it and hold steady to count as found
- GA14 [level-keep](level-keep/) — Keep your head level; tilt and the ball drifts; hold a full 10 seconds to win
- GA15 [slow-scan](slow-scan/) — Scan slowly from left to right; too fast and voice corrects you
- GA16 [nod-quiz](nod-quiz/) — True/false questions: nod for yes, turn and hold steady for no, scored
- GA17 [breathe-turn](breathe-turn/) — Turn slowly as you inhale, return slowly as you exhale, 3 times on each side
- GA18 [guess-direction](guess-direction/) — Guess the secret direction; voice says nearer or farther until you land on it
- GA19 [traffic-light](traffic-light/) — Green light: turn to the announced direction; red light: hold perfectly still
- GA20 [daily-routine](daily-routine/) — Nod, turn, ear-tilt, look up and down, chin tuck — a full set done in 2 minutes
- GA21 [angle-match](angle-match/) — An angle is given for one side; turn into the ±5° band and hold steady to match
- GA22 [tilt-match](tilt-match/) — Ear-tilt to match a small angle; enter the ±5° band and hold steady to match
- GA23 [pace-turn](pace-turn/) — A single head turn taking 3 to 5 seconds counts as even-paced; too fast or too slow does not count
- GA24 [stop-center](stop-center/) — Return from one side and stop within ±5° of center; overshoot is an error; alternate sides 8 times
- GA25 [hold-ramp](hold-ramp/) — Head-down holds ramp up 2/3/4/5/6 seconds level by level, returning to center at each level
- GA26 [memory-six](memory-six/) — Six-direction sequences of 3/4/5 steps; memorize and reproduce in order
- GA41 [snap-hunt](snap-hunt/) — A target object appears on screen; find it in the real environment and photograph it; the large model judges whether it is there
- GA42 [color-catch](color-catch/) — Each round shows a color; photograph a real object whose main color matches, for judgment
- GA43 [word-spot](word-spot/) — Find the target character on signs and packaging and photograph it; the judged text is read out so you can verify
- GA44 [barcode-bingo](barcode-bingo/) — Find barcodes of different symbologies on real objects and scan them into a 3x3 bingo card to make lines
- GA45 [scene-memory](scene-memory/) — Take one panorama photo and get 3 memory questions; turn your head and answer from memory
- GA46 [frame-align](frame-align/) — Move the specified object into the specified cell of a nine-grid and photograph it; judgment reports positions for comparison
- GA47 [odd-one-out](odd-one-out/) — Set out 3 of one kind and 1 odd one; guess its position first, then photograph to reveal the judgment
- GA48 [size-sort](size-sort/) — Set out 3 objects of one kind, order them by size first, then photograph for a full-order comparison judgment
- GA49 [texture-hunt](texture-hunt/) — Each round shows a texture; get close to a real surface and photograph it for texture judgment
- GA50 [snap-diff](snap-diff/) — Shoot the same scene twice; declare the change type first, then dual-image judgment (series finale)
