# Head-Motion Mini-Games (games/)

[中文](README.md) | English | [日本語](README.ja.md)
Translated from the Chinese version (as of 2026-10-29).

Head-motion and photo-interaction mini-games · IDs GA01–GA50: head-motion games GA01–GA40, photo games GA41–GA50. Bare-bones little games you play by moving your head or snapping photos of the world around you.

- GA01 [nod-count](nod-count/) — Voice-guided slow nods, 10 of them; down and back to center counts as one
- GA02 [turn-count](turn-count/) — Voice-guided head turns, alternating left and right, 5 each for 10 total
- GA03 [ear-tilt](ear-tilt/) — Ear-to-shoulder tilts, 3 per side; hold steady 5 seconds for one to count
- GA04 [stretch-hold](stretch-hold/) — Alternate up-and-down stretches, 5-second holds, 3 each way
- GA05 [neck-clock](neck-clock/) — Turn your head to the clock positions as they're called out; come back to center at every point
- GA06 [chin-tuck](chin-tuck/) — Chin-tuck routine: small chin retractions held 5 seconds, 8 reps
- GA07 [steady-hold](steady-hold/) — Hold your head dead still for a full 10 seconds to win; move, and the timer restarts
- GA08 [nod-catch](nod-catch/) — 10 balls drop; nod as they cross the line to catch them and score
- GA09 [turn-dodge](turn-dodge/) — Obstacle on the left? Dodge right. Back to center every round, 10 rounds, scored
- GA10 [follow-dot](follow-dot/) — The light dot jumps — follow it; settle on it to score a hit
- GA11 [rhythm-nod](rhythm-nod/) — A fixed 16-beat rhythm; nod on the beat to score
- GA12 [memory-seq](memory-seq/) — A direction sequence is read out; memorize it and play it back in order
- GA13 [find-target](find-target/) — A direction is called; turn to it and hold steady — that's a find
- GA14 [level-keep](level-keep/) — Keep your head level; tilt, and the ball rolls off; 10 steady seconds wins
- GA15 [slow-scan](slow-scan/) — One slow scan from left to right; go too fast and a voice corrects you
- GA16 [nod-quiz](nod-quiz/) — True-or-false quiz: nod for yes, turn and hold steady for no — scored
- GA17 [breathe-turn](breathe-turn/) — Breathe in and turn slowly, breathe out and come back slowly, 3 times each side
- GA18 [guess-direction](guess-direction/) — Guess the hidden direction; the voice calls warmer or colder until you land on it
- GA19 [traffic-light](traffic-light/) — Green light: turn to the direction called. Red light: freeze
- GA20 [daily-routine](daily-routine/) — Nods, turns, ear tilts, up-and-down, chin tucks — the whole routine in 2 minutes
- GA21 [angle-match](angle-match/) — An angle is called for one side; turn into the ±5° band and settle there to match
- GA22 [tilt-match](tilt-match/) — Ear-tilt to a small target angle; settle inside the ±5° band to match
- GA23 [pace-turn](pace-turn/) — A single turn has to take 3 to 5 seconds to count as even-paced; too fast or too slow doesn't count
- GA24 [stop-center](stop-center/) — Come back from one side and stop within ±5° of center; overshoot is a miss; alternate sides, 8 times
- GA25 [hold-ramp](hold-ramp/) — Head-down holds climb 2/3/4/5/6 seconds, level by level, back to center after each
- GA26 [memory-six](memory-six/) — Six-direction sequences of 3, 4, or 5 steps; memorize them and replay in order
- GA41 [snap-hunt](snap-hunt/) — A target object appears on screen; find it in the real world, photo it, and the model judges whether it's actually there
- GA42 [color-catch](color-catch/) — Each round names a color; photo a real object whose dominant color matches and get it judged
- GA43 [word-spot](word-spot/) — Find the target characters on signs and packaging and photo them; what it read is read back so you can double-check
- GA44 [barcode-bingo](barcode-bingo/) — Hunt down barcodes of different symbologies on real products and scan them into a 3×3 bingo card to complete lines
- GA45 [scene-memory](scene-memory/) — Take one panoramic photo and get 3 memory questions about it; answer from memory as you turn your head
- GA46 [frame-align](frame-align/) — Move the named object into the named cell of a 3×3 grid and photo it; the judgment reports positions so you can compare
- GA47 [odd-one-out](odd-one-out/) — Lay out 3 of a kind plus 1 odd one; guess which it is first, then photo to reveal the verdict
- GA48 [size-sort](size-sort/) — Lay out 3 similar objects, order them by size first, then photo for a full-order comparison
- GA49 [texture-hunt](texture-hunt/) — Each round names a texture; get up close to a real surface, photo it, and get judged on the texture
- GA50 [snap-diff](snap-diff/) — Shoot the same scene twice; declare the type of change first, then both photos are judged together (series finale)
