# Filter (filter/)

[中文](README.md) | English | [日本語](README.ja.md)
Translated from the Chinese version (as of 2026-10-29).

Attention add-on series · IDs FI01–FI05 · 5 agents. Only the one kind of thing you asked for gets through.

- FI01 [focus-shield](focus-shield/) — Only whitelisted sounds get through; everything else goes onto a timeline and is summarized when you take a break
- FI02 [sign-filter](sign-filter/) — Declare your target first; as you scan the street, only matching signs light up
- FI03 [buy-filter](buy-filter/) — Three filters — shopping list, budget, and dietary restrictions; only products that pass all three light up
- FI04 [meeting-filter](meeting-filter/) — Sits through long meetings with you; the screen lights up only when your name or a keyword comes up
- FI05 [lecture-filter](lecture-filter/) — Sits through lectures; whenever a cue sentence lands, it saves a note

For the full list, see the Agent index at the end of the [root README](../README.md).
