# Ambient (ambient/)

[中文](README.md) | English | [日本語](README.ja.md)
Translated from the Chinese version (as of 2026-10-29).

Attention add-on series · IDs AM01–AM05 · 5 agents. The environment never sits still — these keep an eye on it for you.

- AM01 [light-change](light-change/) — Timed light checks with the camera; nudges you to turn a light on when it gets dark
- AM02 [sky-watch](sky-watch/) — Registers your laundry-drying spot, then keeps checking the sky and warns you when clouds roll in
- AM03 [noise-map](noise-map/) — Turn your head to scan 8 directions; it reports the quietest heading
- AM04 [trail-back](trail-back/) — Drops a breadcrumb at every intersection on the way out, then leads you back point by point in reverse
- AM05 [tomorrow-stage](tomorrow-stage/) — Before bed, register what you'll need in the morning; check each item off as you head out

For the full list, see the Agent index at the end of the [root README](../README.md).
