# Ambient (ambient/)

[中文](README.md) | English | [日本語](README.ja.md)
Translated from the Chinese version (as of 2026-10-29).

Attention add-on series · IDs AM01–AM05, 5 in total. The environment keeps changing; it keeps an eye on it for you.

- AM01 [light-change](light-change/) — Camera measures light on a timer; reminds you to turn on the lights when it gets dark
- AM02 [sky-watch](sky-watch/) — Registers the laundry area; checks the sky on a timer and warns of cloud changes
- AM03 [noise-map](noise-map/) — Turn your head to scan 8 directions and report the quietest heading
- AM04 [trail-back](trail-back/) — Registers intersection breadcrumbs along the way; on the return, leads you back point by point in reverse
- AM05 [tomorrow-stage](tomorrow-stage/) — Before bed, register what you need tomorrow morning; on leaving, confirm item by item that all is packed

For the full list, see the Agent list at the end of the [repository root README](../README.md).
