# Social (social/)

[中文](README.md) | English | [日本語](README.ja.md)
Translated from the Chinese version (as of 2026-10-29).

Attention add-on series · IDs SO01–SO05 · 5 agents. It shoulders part of the attention load in social situations.

- SO01 [chat-catchup](chat-catchup/) — Zoned out of the conversation? Shake your head for help and get the gist of the last 15 seconds in front of your eyes
- SO02 [guest-watch](guest-watch/) — Register each guest's seat with a look; timed reminders to glance back at the tea and water
- SO03 [elder-ear](elder-ear/) — Listens in on your chat with an elder; anything phrased as an instruction goes on a list and is read back once the chat is over
- SO04 [standup-echo](standup-echo/) — Listens in standups and lights up only items related to you; the rest is quietly logged and summarized for later
- SO05 [gift-clue](gift-clue/) — Quietly turns "I want…" remarks from everyday chat into clue cards, replayable person by person

For the full list, see the Agent index at the end of the [root README](../README.md).
