# Social (social/)

[中文](README.md) | English | [日本語](README.ja.md)
Translated from the Chinese version (as of 2026-10-29).

Attention add-on series · IDs SO01–SO05, 5 in total. It shares the social attention load for you.

- SO01 [chat-catchup](chat-catchup/) — Lost the thread of the conversation? Shake your head for help and get a summary of the last 15 seconds in view
- SO02 [guest-watch](guest-watch/) — Register each guest's seat by looking; timed reminders to check tea and water levels
- SO03 [elder-ear](elder-ear/) — Listens to an elder's chat; instruction sentences are saved to a list and repeated back after the chat
- SO04 [standup-echo](standup-echo/) — Listens in standups and lights only related sentences; non-hits are silently logged and summarized for later lookup
- SO05 [gift-clue](gift-clue/) — Silently notes "want" sentences in daily chat as clue cards, replayed per person

For the full list, see the Agent list at the end of the [repository root README](../README.md).
