# Cover (cover/)

[中文](README.md) | English | [日本語](README.ja.md)
Translated from the Chinese version (as of 2026-10-29).

Attention add-on series · IDs CO01–CO05, 5 in total. It proves that everything has been seen.

- CO01 [last-glance](last-glance/) — Look around before leaving and archive the last view; replay by zone when anxious
- CO02 [room-sweep](room-sweep/) — Bedtime room check item by item with ticks; missed items reminded by direction to look again
- CO03 [proof-sweep](proof-sweep/) — Finger-read line by line and log progress; skipped lines or passages flagged on the spot, with a coverage rate
- CO04 [pack-sweep](pack-sweep/) — Look at each item on the list to register it; before leaving, it names the few not yet packed
- CO05 [shelf-sweep](shelf-sweep/) — Log scanned shelf sections by layer and segment; unscanned sections are named as reminders

For the full list, see the Agent list at the end of the [repository root README](../README.md).
