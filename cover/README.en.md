# Cover (cover/)

[中文](README.md) | English | [日本語](README.ja.md)
Translated from the Chinese version (as of 2026-10-29).

Attention add-on series · IDs CO01–CO05 · 5 agents. It proves you've actually looked at everything.

- CO01 [last-glance](last-glance/) — Before you leave, look around and archive the last view; replay it zone by zone when you're anxious
- CO02 [room-sweep](room-sweep/) — Bedtime room check, item by item with ticks; anything missed is pointed out by direction so you can look again
- CO03 [proof-sweep](proof-sweep/) — Finger-read line by line and log your progress; skipped lines or sections are flagged on the spot, with a coverage score
- CO04 [pack-sweep](pack-sweep/) — Look at each item on the list to register it as packed; before you leave, it names the ones that aren't in yet
- CO05 [shelf-sweep](shelf-sweep/) — Logs the shelf sections you've scanned, tier by tier and segment by segment; names the ones you missed

For the full list, see the Agent index at the end of the [root README](../README.md).
