# Directional (directional/)

[中文](README.md) | English | [日本語](README.ja.md)
Translated from the Chinese version (as of 2026-10-29).

Directional series · IDs DI01–DI50, 50 in total. Uses directional capabilities such as gaze-coaxial alignment and head orientation to solve everyday problems.

- DI01 [gaze-translate](gaze-translate/) — Look at foreign text and hold your head still; telephoto shot, translate and read aloud
- DI02 [shelf-compare](shelf-compare/) — Scan shelf barcodes into a table to compare prices; nod to add to the list
- DI03 [expiry-check](expiry-check/) — Read package dates, compute days left, voice warning when near expiry
- DI04 [plant-doctor](plant-doctor/) — Photo-diagnose plant condition; voice-guided care steps
- DI05 [manual-lens](manual-lens/) — Read nameplate fault codes and give the next operating step
- DI06 [head-nav](head-nav/) — Navigation arrow turns with your head; walk only when aligned
- DI07 [find-my-thing](find-my-thing/) — Register an item by looking at it and speaking; recall its place and direction
- DI08 [meet-point](meet-point/) — Shared location; turn your head to face a separated friend
- DI09 [parking-return](parking-return/) — Save parking spot location and heading; guided return to your car
- DI10 [exit-radar](exit-radar/) — Look around to register exit directions; announce the nearest exit in an emergency
- DI11 [cook-along](cook-along/) — Recipe floats in view; nod to advance steps, hands-free amid the cooking fumes
- DI12 [repair-buddy](repair-buddy/) — Photograph the faulty part; step-by-step repair guidance, nod to confirm each step
- DI13 [assemble-guide](assemble-guide/) — Match parts against the drawing; guided assembly with error correction
- DI14 [garden-hands](garden-hands/) — Look at a plant for pruning and watering instructions, hands-free
- DI15 [baby-care-hands](baby-care-hands/) — Hands-free voice Q&A while holding the baby; photograph to ask about the baby's state
- DI16 [sound-compass](sound-compass/) — Turn your head to scan for a sound source and report which side it is on
- DI17 [far-ear](far-ear/) — Far-field audio pickup to live captions; hear distant voices clearly
- DI18 [cry-watch](cry-watch/) — Keeps watch over the nursery; voice alert the moment crying is heard
- DI19 [name-call](name-call/) — Hear who is calling you; report content and direction together
- DI20 [ambient-captions](ambient-captions/) — Omnidirectional pickup with live captions; whoever you face gets enhanced
- DI21 [market-radar](market-radar/) — Register stall prices by heading; turn your head to light up prices and compare
- DI22 [allergen-guard](allergen-guard/) — Read ingredient lists for allergens; instant warning on a match
- DI23 [fresh-pick](fresh-pick/) — Photograph to judge fruit and vegetable ripeness and freshness, with picking advice
- DI24 [outfit-check](outfit-check/) — Photograph to rate outfit coordination and occasion fit; rate again in the mirror on-body
- DI25 [receipt-keeper](receipt-keeper/) — One glance logs a receipt; monthly summary by voice
- DI26 [home-find](home-find/) — Register where things are stored; when searching, report direction clues
- DI27 [meter-reader](meter-reader/) — Telephoto-read water and electricity meters; auto-log and flag anomalies
- DI28 [home-patrol](home-patrol/) — Inspect doors, windows and gas before leaving; each item must be seen before you can go
- DI29 [parcel-sort](parcel-sort/) — Scan parcel waybill barcodes; sort piles by urgency with voice guidance
- DI30 [clean-check](clean-check/) — Look around to log cleaning coverage; remind you of missed corners
- DI31 [med-look](med-look/) — Read medicine bottle labels against the dosing schedule; announce dose and log as taken
- DI32 [posture-guard](posture-guard/) — Measures head-down time; gentle voice reminder after too long
- DI33 [fall-guard](fall-guard/) — If a fall gets no response, contact the preset emergency contact
- DI34 [cross-street](cross-street/) — Before crossing, turn your head left and right and hold steady; only reports safe after double confirmation
- DI35 [food-safe](food-safe/) — Judge leftovers by appearance plus registered date; graded risk warning
- DI36 [word-look](word-look/) — Look at an unfamiliar word and say it; definition and pronunciation go into the word book
- DI37 [story-look](story-look/) — Photograph a picture-book page to generate narration and questions; turn the page to continue
- DI38 [memory-anchor](memory-anchor/) — That thought or to-do from a second ago — forgotten the moment you turn your head
- DI39 [board-capture](board-capture/) — Blackboard and whiteboard writing in class or meetings is far away and erased fast
- DI40 [homework-lens](homework-lens/) — The child can't solve the problem, and the parent can't explain the steps clearly
- DI41 [sign-reader](sign-reader/) — Stop signs, departure boards and menus too far or too small to read
- DI42 [transit-direction](transit-direction/) — Calibrate direction by turning your head after leaving the subway station; voice correction when you drift
- DI43 [hotel-checkin](hotel-checkin/) — Check eight checkpoints one by one; missed ones are led back to by direction
- DI44 [photo-level](photo-level/) — Rule-of-thirds grid plus bubble level overlay; nod to shoot

- DI46 [speaker-focus](speaker-focus/) — Can't follow who is speaking in a multi-person meeting; minutes tag speakers by heading
- DI47 [badge-reader](badge-reader/) — Can't read a name badge and forgot the name? Telephoto reads it and quietly tells you

- DI49 [site-patrol](site-patrol/) — Site and warehouse patrol needs item-by-item looking and logging, with both hands holding tools
