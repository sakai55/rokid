# Directional (directional/)

[中文](README.md) | English | [日本語](README.ja.md)
Translated from the Chinese version (as of 2026-10-29).

Directional series · IDs DI01–DI50 · 50 agents. They put gaze-axis alignment and head direction to work on everyday problems.

- DI01 [gaze-translate](gaze-translate/) — Look at foreign text and hold your head still; a telephoto shot gets it translated and read aloud
- DI02 [shelf-compare](shelf-compare/) — Scan shelf barcodes into a price-comparison table; nod to add an item to your list
- DI03 [expiry-check](expiry-check/) — Reads the date on the package, counts the days left, and warns you out loud when expiry is near
- DI04 [plant-doctor](plant-doctor/) — Photo a plant to diagnose its condition; the care steps are talked through out loud
- DI05 [manual-lens](manual-lens/) — Reads the fault code off the nameplate and tells you what to do next
- DI06 [head-nav](head-nav/) — The nav arrow turns with your head; you only move when it's lined up
- DI07 [find-my-thing](find-my-thing/) — Look at an item and say its name to register it; later it recalls where it is and which way to face
- DI08 [meet-point](meet-point/) — Shares locations; just turn your head to face the friend you got separated from
- DI09 [parking-return](parking-return/) — Saves your parking spot and the way you were facing; guides you back to the car
- DI10 [exit-radar](exit-radar/) — Look around to register where the exits are; in an emergency it announces the nearest one
- DI11 [cook-along](cook-along/) — The recipe floats in view; nod to advance a step — and it picks out your voice even over the cooking fumes
- DI12 [repair-buddy](repair-buddy/) — Photo the broken part; it walks you through the repair step by step, nod to confirm each one
- DI13 [assemble-guide](assemble-guide/) — Line the parts up against the diagram; step-by-step assembly guidance that catches mistakes
- DI14 [garden-hands](garden-hands/) — Look at a plant and get pruning and watering instructions — fully hands-free
- DI15 [baby-care-hands](baby-care-hands/) — Voice Q&A while your hands are full of baby; photo the baby to ask how they're doing
- DI16 [sound-compass](sound-compass/) — Turn your head to scan for where a sound is coming from; it reports which side it's on
- DI17 [far-ear](far-ear/) — Far-field pickup turned into live captions, so distant voices come through clearly
- DI18 [cry-watch](cry-watch/) — Keeps an ear on the nursery; the moment crying starts, you get a voice alert
- DI19 [name-call](name-call/) — Hears who is calling your name and reports what they said and from which direction
- DI20 [ambient-captions](ambient-captions/) — Omnidirectional pickup with live captions; whoever you're facing gets boosted
- DI21 [market-radar](market-radar/) — Register stall prices by the direction you face; turn your head to light a price up and compare
- DI22 [allergen-guard](allergen-guard/) — Reads ingredient lists and checks them against your allergens; a hit gets an instant warning
- DI23 [fresh-pick](fresh-pick/) — Photo produce to judge ripeness and freshness — like having someone who knows produce standing next to you
- DI24 [outfit-check](outfit-check/) — Photo an outfit for a verdict on the match and the occasion; try it on and get rated in the mirror, too
- DI25 [receipt-keeper](receipt-keeper/) — One glance at a receipt and it's logged; the monthly roundup is read out to you
- DI26 [home-find](home-find/) — Register where things go when you put them away; when you hunt for something, it gives you direction clues
- DI27 [meter-reader](meter-reader/) — Reads water and power meters by telephoto; logs readings automatically and flags anything abnormal
- DI28 [home-patrol](home-patrol/) — Before you leave, check doors, windows, and gas item by item — everything has to be seen before you're cleared to go
- DI29 [parcel-sort](parcel-sort/) — Scan parcel waybill barcodes; it sorts them into piles by urgency, calling out where each one goes
- DI30 [clean-check](clean-check/) — Look around to log what's been cleaned; it reminds you about the corners you missed
- DI31 [med-look](med-look/) — Reads the label on the medicine bottle against your dosing schedule; calls out the dose and logs it as taken
- DI32 [posture-guard](posture-guard/) — Tracks how long your head stays down; a gentle voice nudge when it's been too long
- DI33 [fall-guard](fall-guard/) — If you fall and don't respond, it contacts your preset emergency contact
- DI34 [cross-street](cross-street/) — Before you cross, turn your head left and right and hold steady; it only calls it safe after both sides check out
- DI35 [food-safe](food-safe/) — Judges leftovers by how they look plus when you registered them, and gives a graded risk warning
- DI36 [word-look](word-look/) — Look at a word you don't know and say it; the definition and pronunciation land in your word book
- DI37 [story-look](story-look/) — Photo a picture-book page and it generates narration plus questions; turn the page and it keeps going
- DI38 [memory-anchor](memory-anchor/) — That idea — or to-do — you had a second ago, gone the moment you turn your head
- DI39 [board-capture](board-capture/) — Blackboard and whiteboard writing in class and meetings: too far away to read, and erased too fast
- DI40 [homework-lens](homework-lens/) — Your kid is stuck on a problem, and you can't explain the steps clearly
- DI41 [sign-reader](sign-reader/) — Bus-stop signs, departure boards, menus — too far away, type too small to read
- DI42 [transit-direction](transit-direction/) — Out of the subway station, turn your head to calibrate your bearings; drift off course and a voice corrects you
- DI43 [hotel-checkin](hotel-checkin/) — Eight checkpoints, each looked at and ticked off; miss one and it leads you back to it by direction
- DI44 [photo-level](photo-level/) — Rule-of-thirds grid plus a bubble level in the viewfinder; nod to fire the shutter
- DI45 [queue-sense](queue-sense/) — Counts heads by eye and gauges the line's speed; estimates which queue will move fastest
- DI46 [speaker-focus](speaker-focus/) — Can't track who's talking in a group meeting? The minutes tag each speaker by the direction you faced
- DI47 [badge-reader](badge-reader/) — Can't make out a name badge — or forgot the name? Telephoto reads it and whispers it to you
- DI48 [teleprompter-gaze](teleprompter-gaze/) — Losing your lines in a speech or on video, and glancing down at notes looks unnatural
- DI49 [site-patrol](site-patrol/) — Site and warehouse patrols mean looking at and logging every item, both hands busy with tools
- DI50 [elder-day](elder-day/) — A daily checklist for an older adult living alone: medication, water, heading out — confirmed one item at a time
