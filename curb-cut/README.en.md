# Curb Cut (curb-cut/)

[中文](README.md) | English | [日本語](README.ja.md)
Translated from the Chinese version (as of 2026-10-29).

Curb-cut series · IDs CC01–CC20 (2 present in this directory so far; the rest are being completed). Solve one specific problem for one group with special needs first, then let it spill over to ordinary people in situational difficulty.

- CC01 [drop-search](drop-search/) — A small dropped item: feel through the 6 zones around your feet zone by zone; only reports "not found" after every zone is checked
- CC02 [my-bus-watch](my-bus-watch/) — Watches only your one registered bus route; photograph the front to verify the route number and report whether to board

For the full list, see the Agent list at the end of the [repository root README](../README.md).
