# Curb Cut (curb-cut/)

[中文](README.md) | English | [日本語](README.ja.md)
Translated from the Chinese version (as of 2026-10-29).

Curb-cut series · IDs CC01–CC20 (2 in this directory so far; the rest are on the way). Each one first solves a specific problem for a specific group with a disability — and then turns out to help anyone in the same situational bind.

- CC01 [drop-search](drop-search/) — Dropped something small? Feel through the 6 zones around your feet, zone by zone; it only reports "not found" once every zone has been checked
- CC02 [my-bus-watch](my-bus-watch/) — Watches for your one registered bus route only; photo the front of the bus to verify the route number and hear whether to board

For the full list, see the Agent index at the end of the [root README](../README.md).
