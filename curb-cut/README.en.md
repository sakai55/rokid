# Curb Cut (curb-cut/)

[中文](README.md) | English | [日本語](README.ja.md)
Translated from the Chinese version (as of 2026-10-29).

Curb-cut series · IDs CC01–CC20 (17 in this directory). Each one first solves a specific problem for a specific group with a disability — and then turns out to help anyone in the same situational bind.

- CC01 [drop-search](drop-search/) — A small dropped item: feel through the six zones around your feet one by one; it reports not found only after checking them all
- CC02 [my-bus-watch](my-bus-watch/) — Watches only your one registered bus route; photographs the front to check the route number and reports whether you boarded
- CC03 [step-count-down](step-count-down/) — Count down stairs step by step; spoken countdown starts three steps from the bottom
- CC04 [color-order-check](color-order-check/) — Call out wire colors one by one; the order is checked to prevent miswiring
- CC05 [greet-name-hold](greet-name-hold/) — Catches names during small talk and holds them in the corner of your view
- CC06 [alarm-triage-visual](alarm-triage-visual/) — For deaf users: household sounds become visual banners, triaged
- CC07 [last-sentence](last-sentence/) — Lost the thread of a group chat: freeze and replay the last sentence
- CC08 [curb-ramp-scout](curb-ramp-scout/) — Wheelchair users: know before the crossing whether the ramp is on the left or right
- CC09 [high-shelf-ask](high-shelf-ask/) — Cannot reach a high shelf: lock onto the item and ask staff to fetch it
- CC10 [freeze-step-cue](freeze-step-cue/) — Freezing at the doorway: one beat per step gets you walking
- CC11 [left-home-proof](left-home-proof/) — Worried about the gas or locks after leaving: replay the saved proof to end the anxiety
- CC12 [retrace-steps](retrace-steps/) — Forgot how you got here: walk back along your own steps in reverse
- CC13 [task-anchor](task-anchor/) — Attention anchor: one task stays pinned, with light reminders per segment
- CC14 [quiet-corner](quiet-corner/) — Sensory overload: a single sentence pointing to the quietest direction
- CC15 [line-by-line](line-by-line/) — Reading support: one large line at a time, nod to move to the next line
- CC16 [say-by-looking](say-by-looking/) — Speaks for people with aphasia: nod to pick one of four phrases, TTS says it
- CC17 [first-sentence](first-sentence/) — Speaking with a stutter: the first sentence shown word by word at a set pace

For the full list, see the Agent index at the end of the [root README](../README.md).
