Attention add-on series · IDs RE01–RE05, 5 in total. It answers "just now…" for you.

- RE01 [day-lookback](day-lookback/) — Daytime gazes are laid out by time and heading; follow the line to review at night
- RE02 [talk-replay](talk-replay/) — Conversation captions by speaker heading; replay lists only promises, numbers and deadlines
- RE03 [shop-replay](shop-replay/) — Hesitated-over products archived by storefront heading; replay compares prices and pins the cheapest
- RE04 [route-replay](route-replay/) — Danger points registered on the road; before you head out the next day they are announced in order
- RE05 [attention-diary](attention-diary/) — Who stole your attention all day, spelled out in one bill
