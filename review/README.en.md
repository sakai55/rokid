# Review (review/)

[中文](README.md) | English | [日本語](README.ja.md)
Translated from the Chinese version (as of 2026-10-29).

Attention add-on series · IDs RE01–RE05 · 5 agents. It answers "wait, what just happened?" for you.

- RE01 [day-lookback](day-lookback/) — Daytime gazes are laid out along a line by time and heading; walk the line at night to review your day
- RE02 [talk-replay](talk-replay/) — Conversation captions tagged by speaker direction; replay lists nothing but promises, numbers, and deadlines
- RE03 [shop-replay](shop-replay/) — Products you hesitated over are archived by storefront direction; replay compares prices and pins the cheapest
- RE04 [route-replay](route-replay/) — Register hazards along your route; next morning it previews them in order before you head out
- RE05 [attention-diary](attention-diary/) — Who stole your attention today, itemized in a single bill

For the full list, see the Agent index at the end of the [root README](../README.md).
