# Dual (dual/)

[中文](README.md) | English | [日本語](README.ja.md)
Translated from the Chinese version (as of 2026-10-29).

Attention add-on series · IDs DU01–DU05 · 5 agents. You run the main thread; it babysits the side one.

- DU01 [play-watch](play-watch/) — Playground side-watch: timed reminders to look back; unacknowledged alerts escalate step by step
- DU02 [bag-watch](bag-watch/) — Luggage side-watch: timed reminders to look back; head for the door without confirming and it calls you to stop
- DU03 [dog-walk](dog-walk/) — Dog-walk side-watch: when your dog stops, it classifies what for on the spot; at your front door it checks items one by one before letting you in
- DU04 [tour-watch](tour-watch/) — Tour-group side-watch: it picks out regroup calls only; as the meet time nears, it points you back to the group by direction
- DU05 [queue-roam](queue-roam/) — Queue-number side-watch: it listens for your number, and once yours is within 5, it calls you back to wait

For the full list, see the Agent index at the end of the [root README](../README.md).
