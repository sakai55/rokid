# Dual (dual/)

[中文](README.md) | English | [日本語](README.ja.md)
Translated from the Chinese version (as of 2026-10-29).

Attention add-on series · IDs DU01–DU05, 5 in total. You run the main line; it handles the side line.

- DU01 [play-watch](play-watch/) — A side-line timer reminds you to check the playground; unacknowledged alerts escalate step by step
- DU02 [bag-watch](bag-watch/) — A side-line timer reminds you to check your luggage; trying to leave the door unconfirmed triggers a stop call
- DU03 [dog-walk](dog-walk/) — On the dog-walking side line, when the dog stops it classifies on the spot; at the home door, items are checked one by one before entry
- DU04 [tour-watch](tour-watch/) — On the tour side line, only gathering calls are picked out; near the time, it reminds you by direction to rejoin the group
- DU05 [queue-roam](queue-roam/) — The number-taking side line listens for your call; within 5 numbers it calls you back to wait

For the full list, see the Agent list at the end of the [repository root README](../README.md).
