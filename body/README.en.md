# Body (body/)

[中文](README.md) | English | [日本語](README.ja.md)
Translated from the Chinese version (as of 2026-10-29).

Attention add-on series · IDs BO01–BO05, 5 in total. The signals your body sends, it keeps for you.

- BO01 [eye-rest](eye-rest/) — Head held steady gazing near for a full 20 minutes triggers a reminder to look far; confirm by raising your head
- BO02 [water-watch](water-watch/) — If you haven't looked at your cup for a long time, the screen lights up to remind you to drink; confirm by gazing
- BO03 [breath-pace](breath-pace/) — A breathing-pace light dot in view; just follow it with your eyes
- BO04 [stand-move](stand-move/) — Sitting past the threshold triggers a stand-up reminder; look around once to confirm
- BO05 [snack-aware](snack-aware/) — Looking at a snack first shows how many times today; whether to eat is your call

For the full list, see the Agent list at the end of the [repository root README](../README.md).
