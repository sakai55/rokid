# Body (body/)

[中文](README.md) | English | [日本語](README.ja.md)
Translated from the Chinese version (as of 2026-10-29).

Attention add-on series · IDs BO01–BO05 · 5 agents. Your body sends signals all day — these catch them for you.

- BO01 [eye-rest](eye-rest/) — Hold your head steady on something near for a full 20 minutes and it reminds you to look into the distance; raise your head to confirm
- BO02 [water-watch](water-watch/) — Haven't glanced at your water cup in a while? The screen lights up to remind you to drink; gaze to confirm
- BO03 [breath-pace](breath-pace/) — A light dot pulses at breathing pace in your view — just follow it with your eyes
- BO04 [stand-move](stand-move/) — Sitting past the threshold triggers a stand-up reminder; look around once to confirm
- BO05 [snack-aware](snack-aware/) — Look at a snack and it first shows how many times you've had one today; whether to eat is your call

For the full list, see the Agent index at the end of the [root README](../README.md).
