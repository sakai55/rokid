Japan–Korea localization series · IDs KR01–KR10 · 10 agents. Agents localized for everyday scenes in Korea.

- KR01 [samgyeop-flip](samgyeop-flip/) — Samgyeopsal flipping rhythm; it calls out the moment to flip the pork belly
- KR02 [noise-diary](noise-diary/) — Logs only your own home's sounds at night; play them back in the morning and check for yourself
- KR03 [soju-turn](soju-turn/) — Soju etiquette, choreographed: pour with two hands, turn aside to drink
- KR04 [jongnyang-gauge](jongnyang-gauge/) — Volume-based trash bags: a gauge for the bag's marked capacity and a ledger of what you've thrown out
- KR05 [hangang-base](hangang-base/) — Hangang campsite base: pickup codes, food order numbers, and trash returns — all kept in one place
- KR06 [deungsan-pace](deungsan-pace/) — Hiking pace ledger: water, rest stops, and the descent, registered as you go
- KR07 [noraebang-rotation](noraebang-rotation/) — Noraebang rotation: a fair running order of singers and song numbers
- KR08 [kimjang-batch](kimjang-batch/) — Kimjang season: cabbage counts by the head and by the batch, tallied in big type
- KR09 [jjimjil-rest](jjimjil-rest/) — Jjimjilbang rest timer: alternates the hot–cold cycles, with no claims about health effects
- KR10 [baedal-code](baedal-code/) — Delivery door codes and order numbers, revealed character by character and forgotten the moment they're used

For the full list, see the Agent index at the end of the [root README](../README.md).
